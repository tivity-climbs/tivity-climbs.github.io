ORBIT COURIER

You fly the last parcel skiff in the Vesta–Korr system. Planets are too heavy to land on — their gravity is your engine. Slingshot between moons and set packages down softly.

Goal: make 5 deliveries. Each delivery: land on the glowing target moon with a relative speed under 60. Touching a planet or landing too fast = crash (you lose). Running dry in open space or drifting out of the system also loses.

  • Keyboard: ←→ or AD rotate, ↑ / W / Space thrust, ↓ / S retro-thrust
  • Mouse / touch: press and hold anywhere — the ship turns toward your finger/cursor and burns
  • Thrusting burns fuel. Each delivery refuels +45%. Faster deliveries & spare fuel score more.
  • The dotted line predicts your path for the next few seconds. Red ✕ = impact.
  • R restart any time · P pause · M mute
