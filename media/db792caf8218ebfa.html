ORBIT COURIER

A courier run across the Aurelia system — deliver 8 packages between the moons.
Mission: fly to the pulsing pickup beacon, grab the package, then deliver it to the target moon marked by the golden arrow. Repeat 8 times to win.
Thrust burns fuel — fuel is scarce. Skim the dashed scoop ring around a planet (don't get closer!) to refuel. Plan gravity slingshots: falling toward a body bends your path for free.
