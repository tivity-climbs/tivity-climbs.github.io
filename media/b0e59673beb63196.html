freight license · class C

Five parcels.
No brakes.
Gravity is your engine.

You fly the only freighter between the moons of Kestrel. Thrust burns fuel, so you burn it in short bursts and let the planets bend your path. Collect a parcel from the lit moon, carry it to the second one, do that five times before the fuel and the debris decide otherwise.

Aim with the ribbon

The dotted line is your real future, gravity included. Where it goes, you go.

Approach gently

Touch the lit moon under the speed limit to dock. Arrive fast and the parcel is scrap.

Steal speed

Swing behind a planet to slingshot. Falling is free — thrust is not.

Don't drift

Out of fuel means adrift, and adrift has a countdown. Hit the edge of the system and you're gone.

keyboard WASD · mouse or touch drag · Enter to launch
how to play

Flying the Kestrel line

Objective: complete 5 deliveries. Each delivery refills your fuel and pays a bonus for speed, leftover fuel and a soft dock.

Thrust

WASD or the arrow keys push the ship that way. Hold two for a diagonal.

Pointer / touch

Press and drag anywhere on the field — the stick sets direction and strength. Release to coast.

Brake

Space fires retro thrusters against your velocity. Use it before a dock, not after.

Keys

P pause · R restart · M mute · H this card · Enter confirm.

Holding station

The clock is stopped. Nothing moves while you think.
