Orbit Courier

GRAVITY IS YOUR ENGINE · THRUST IS YOUR EMERGENCY BRAKE
Mission: you are a courier for the outer moons. Fly to the amber moon to pick up a package, then carry it to the green moon to deliver it. Complete 5 deliveries to win. Planets and the sun pull on your ship — coast on their gravity to save fuel (solar panels slowly recharge the tank while you coast). Touching any surface at speed destroys you.
